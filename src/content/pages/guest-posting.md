PawpawDecors welcomes thoughtful pitches for original articles about home decor, gardening, outdoor living and plant care. A useful contribution should answer a real reader question and offer practical detail that is not already covered by the same information across many websites.

## What to send

Email the admin with a proposed headline, a short outline, the intended reader and a brief note about your relevant experience. Include links to one or two examples of your previous work when available. Do not send a full article unless it has been requested.

## Editorial standards

Submissions must be original, accurate and written for readers. We do not publish copied articles, lightly reworded content, keyword-stuffed drafts, product catalogues or promotional press releases. Claims about plants, products and home projects should be specific and supported where appropriate.

PawpawDecors reviews every pitch and may edit accepted work for clarity, accuracy, length and style. Sending a pitch does not guarantee a reply or publication. Unpublished submissions are not returned.

## Links, payment and disclosure

We do not sell dofollow links, promise search-ranking benefits or accept payment in exchange for keyword-rich backlinks. Links must help readers and be relevant to the article. Any commercial relationship, gifted product or affiliate link must be disclosed clearly. Links may be marked with `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"` where appropriate.

Google's spam policies warn against using guest articles primarily to build links. We accept pitches for their usefulness to readers, not as a link-placement service.
