## Who publishes PawpawDecors

PawpawDecors is an independently managed website founded and written by Talha Raziq. He is currently the site's sole author and is responsible for its content and editorial decisions.

## Content and accuracy

Articles are intended to give readers useful ideas and practical information about home decor, gardening and plant care. We aim to explain steps clearly, use descriptive headings and distinguish general guidance from advice that depends on a particular climate, plant variety, product or home.

Plant-care results vary with light, temperature, water quality, soil, pests and local growing conditions. Readers should check advice against the needs of their own plants and seek qualified local help where a problem could cause significant damage.

When research or outside sources inform an article, the content should add explanation or practical context rather than simply copying or rewording another website. We do not promise that every suggestion will work in every home or garden.

## Corrections and updates

We welcome specific corrections. Please email the article URL, the statement in question and a reliable source or explanation. We review reported issues and update an article when a correction or material change is needed. Updated dates are shown when an article has been meaningfully revised.

## Images and attribution

Images should be original, licensed for use or supplied with permission. Captions and meaningful alternative text are used where appropriate. We do not knowingly use images without the right to publish them.

## Affiliate links and advertising

Some articles may contain affiliate links. When they do, a disclosure appears near the article content. PawpawDecors may earn a commission if a reader buys through a qualifying link, at no extra cost to the reader. Affiliate relationships do not guarantee a positive review or determine an editorial conclusion.

Affiliate links should be identified with the appropriate `rel="sponsored"` attribute when added to article content. Advertising, including Google AdSense when enabled, is separate from editorial decisions. Ads do not buy favourable coverage.

## Guest contributions

We review relevant original pitches through the [Guest Posting page](/guest-posting/). We do not sell links, guarantee publication or accept articles created primarily to place keyword-rich backlinks.
