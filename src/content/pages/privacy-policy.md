*Last updated: 13 November 2026*

PawpawDecors is an independently managed website founded and operated by Talha Raziq. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the website or contact its admin.

## Information you provide

You can read the website without creating an account. There is no reader account or on-site comment system.

When you email the site, the message may include your email address, name and any information you choose to share. That information is used to read and respond to your enquiry, handle a correction or consider a relevant collaboration. Your message is also processed by the email providers involved in sending and receiving it.

## Technical information

The website's hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and request times. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive standard technical information when your browser requests those files.

PawpawDecors currently uses browser local storage to remember the light or dark theme preference. This setting is stored on your device and can be cleared in your browser.

## Advertising

When Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, the provider and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to display ads, measure ad performance, prevent invalid activity or personalise ads where allowed by law and the visitor's settings. Google's information about [how it uses data on partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and its [advertising technology policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) explain those practices. You can manage personalisation through [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

## Affiliate links

Some articles may link to third-party retailers or services through affiliate programmes. If you follow one of these links, the third party may use cookies or similar technologies to attribute a purchase or referral. The third party's own privacy policy applies after you leave PawpawDecors.

PawpawDecors may receive limited referral or commission information from an affiliate partner. It does not receive your full payment-card details through an ordinary affiliate link.

## How information is used and shared

Information is used to operate and protect the website, respond to messages, correct content and understand referral activity where an advertising or affiliate provider supplies reports. It may be processed by hosting, email, font, advertising and affiliate service providers as needed for their services. PawpawDecors does not sell personal information to data brokers.

## Retention and your choices

Emails are kept for as long as reasonably needed to handle the enquiry and maintain relevant records. Technical logs and third-party records are handled under the retention practices of the providers involved. You can clear the theme preference through your browser settings and manage cookies through the same controls.

Privacy rights vary by country. Where applicable law gives you rights to access, correct or delete personal information, you can contact the site admin to make a request.

## Children's privacy

PawpawDecors is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Please contact the admin if you believe a child has sent personal information through email.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be revised when the website's services or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.

For privacy questions or requests, email [talharaziq@outlook.com](mailto:talharaziq@outlook.com).
