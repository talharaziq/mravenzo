I’m **Talha Raziq**, the founder and sole author of PawpawDecors. I have a background in biotechnology and a genuine love for plants. I enjoy learning how plants grow, how to care for them and how a little greenery can change the feel of a home.

During my research internship at the **National Institute for Genomics and Advanced Biotechnology (NIGAB)**, I worked on potato gene transfer. That experience gave me a closer look at plant biotechnology and the research behind crop improvement.

I also wrote the review article [“Chloroplast Genetic Engineering in Crop Plants: Techniques and Applications”](https://doi.org/10.31015/2025.si.10), published in the *International Journal of Agriculture, Environment and Food Sciences* in 2025.

PawpawDecors brings that interest in plants together with practical ideas for home decor, gardens and everyday plant care. I write and manage the website independently.
