Publishing notes

  1. Keep an article as a draft while it is being prepared.
  2. Turn on Publish this article when the story is ready.
  3. Use the Featured switch to add a story to the homepage carousel. The four newest published featured stories appear automatically.
  4. Talha Raziq is the default author. No author details need to be entered for new articles.
  5. Complete the featured-image alt text and page-specific SEO title and description before publishing.
